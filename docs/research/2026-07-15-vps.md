# VPS plan research — 2026-07-15

## Decision

Only one current US-market plan was found that matches the hard billing and capacity contract on official published terms: **OVHcloud US VPS-4 2027 in `US-EAST-VA` at $27.50 USD per month before tax**.

The required second qualifying plan was **not found**. Purchase is therefore owner-gated and not authorized by this research. Immediately before any purchase, the owner must inspect the actual checkout and explicitly approve it.

## Contract tested

Every candidate was tested against all of these requirements:

- exactly one new recurring infrastructure bill;
- fixed USD price of $25–$75 per month before tax;
- month-to-month, with no annual commitment or annual prepayment;
- at least 8 shared vCPUs, 24 GiB RAM, and 200 GB NVMe;
- provider-native daily whole-server or volume backup included at no additional recurring cost;
- enough transfer that use cannot create an automatic or usage-priced overage;
- no metered compute, autoscaling, automatic top-up, or separately paid dependency; and
- United States East preferred for the owner-region checkout.

Only official provider pricing, checkout, catalog, documentation, and terms pages were used for the decisions below. Prices and inventory can change, so this is a dated research record rather than standing purchase authorization.

The backup promotion has no listed end date in the catalog, and OVH's public plan pages independently state that the automatic daily backup is included. It must nevertheless remain `$0` in the owner's final cart.

The marketed `$23.37/month` amount is not compliant: the catalog associates it with `upfront12`, commitment `12`, and twelve required repeats. The compliant selection is `default`, commitment `0`, at `$27.50/month`.

### Open verification points

- **GB versus GiB:** OVH publishes `24 GB`; the design target says `24 GiB`. Confirm the allocated byte count with OVH or make post-provision memory measurement a deployment gate. Do not silently equate the labels.
- **Tax:** the public catalog is pre-tax. Applicable sales tax depends on the owner's billing address and must be shown at checkout.
- **Inventory and region:** confirm `US-EAST-VA` is selectable at the time of purchase.
- **Backup:** confirm Standard automatic backup is selected, costs `$0`, runs daily, and can be restored. Do not select paid Premium backup or snapshots.
- **No extras:** Linux, local NVMe storage, setup, and transfer must remain free of additional recurring or usage charges.

## Near matches and conflicts

These are all of the near matches or relevant conflicts recorded during this research. A plan is rejected when any one hard condition is missing or ambiguous; no condition was silently relaxed.

### SERVER1.GE Managed VPS B and VPS A

- VPS B official USD checkout: $58.50 monthly, setup free, 8 vCore, 24 GB RAM, 200 GB NVMe SSD, and traffic advertised as unlimited.
- VPS A official USD checkout: $73.30 monthly, setup free, 12 vCore, 48 GB RAM, 300 GB NVMe SSD, and traffic advertised as unlimited.
- The storefront says every plan includes a daily backup of the previous 24 hours and North America/Europe availability.
- The actual configuration listed Canada, not a named United States East location.
- The generic official VPS FAQ says reaching a traffic limit may cause throttling **or an additional fee after notice**. That leaves a usage-overage path incompatible with the contract unless SERVER1 provides plan-specific written terms superseding it.
- Managed-product root access is not sufficiently clear for the required Docker/Coder host administration.
- Taxes may be added where applicable under the provider terms.

Official sources: [USD storefront](https://console.server1.ge/store/managed-linux-vps?currency=6), [VPS B configuration](https://console.server1.ge/cart.php?a=add&pid=132&billingcycle=monthly&currency=6&language=english), [VPS A configuration](https://console.server1.ge/cart.php?a=add&pid=133&billingcycle=monthly&currency=6&language=english), [VPS and traffic FAQ](https://server1.ge/en/vps), [managed VPS details](https://server1.ge/en/vps/managed-vps), and [terms](https://server1.ge/en/terms-and-conditions).

**Result:** not conclusively compliant. Written confirmation would need to guarantee no transfer fee or top-up, full root/Docker access, exact location, daily whole-server backup at `$0`, binary memory capacity, and the final taxed checkout total.

### Abhax VPS-3 and VPS-4

- Official cart pages showed VPS-3 at $30.77 monthly with 8 vCore, 24 GB, 200 GB NVMe, unlimited traffic, and a daily 24-hour backup; VPS-4 was $53.52 with 12 vCore, 48 GB, and 300 GB NVMe.

- A specific server region and a complete enforceable transfer/overage contract were not established.
- VPS provisioning is described as non-refundable, making the contradictory pre-purchase specifications material.

Official sources: [Linux VPS storefront](https://clientarea.abhax.com/store/linux-vps), [VPS-3 cart](https://clientarea.abhax.com/cart.php?a=add&pid=6&billingcycle=monthly&currency=2), [VPS-4 cart](https://clientarea.abhax.com/cart.php?a=add&pid=7&billingcycle=monthly&currency=2), [main product site](https://abhax.com/), and [refund policy](https://abhax.com/refund_returns-2/).

**Result:** rejected because official same-provider specifications conflict and region/terms are incomplete.

1. Provider: OVHcloud US.
2. Plan: `VPS-4 2027` / `vps-2027-model4`.
3. Region: `US-EAST-VA`.
4. Billing: default monthly, commitment `0`; no upfront term.
5. Base price: $27.50 USD/month.
6. Setup: $0.
7. Linux and local NVMe storage: $0.
8. Standard automatic daily backup: selected and $0.
9. Premium backup, snapshots, extra disks, licenses, and other paid options: absent.
10. Transfer: unlimited US traffic with no paid overage or automatic top-up.
11. Tax and exact final recurring total: visible and accepted by the owner.
12. The 24 GB/GiB caveat: explicitly acknowledged or resolved.

If any value differs, stop and update this research and `COST_MODEL.md`; do not substitute another plan or relax a condition silently.
